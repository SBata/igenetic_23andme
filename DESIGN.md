# Results workspace direction

Modern science dashboard for personal genetic exploration. This is the agent-selected direction following the request for stronger design, with a compact model browser, coverage display and signed contribution bars. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. Existing application navigation stays intact.

- Ink blue anchors the numerical summary; white working surfaces separate the model details from the pale canvas.
- Teal and copper encode lower and higher contributions, always accompanied by words or signs. They are not healthy/unhealthy verdicts.
- System sans-serif makes the results readable at data density; the existing serif wordmark preserves continuity. Monospace is reserved for marker IDs.
- The trait list remains visible beside a selected result on desktop and reflows into a two-column selector on mobile.
- A large subtotal is paired with coverage in the same surface, so a number cannot be read without its completeness context.
- Diverging bars answer which observed variants contribute most, using a zero centre and one scale per selected model. Models are not compared on a shared raw-score axis.
- Short variant explanations precede arithmetic detail. Search, filters and progressive row disclosure keep large models navigable without rendering thousands of rows.
- The light working surface supports long reading and table inspection. Motion is limited to native focus/hover feedback; no decorative assets or animation are needed.
